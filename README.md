## 📚 Research

*All four papers are currently under review — preprints on arXiv.*

**🏥 ShifaMind** — Multiplicative Concept Bottleneck for Interpretable ICD-10 Coding · *submitted NeurIPS 2026*
> 160 learnable concept queries cross-attend over 6,144-token discharge notes via BioClinical-ModernBERT, with **zero residual bypass** — explanations are faithful by construction. Macro-F1 **0.7122** on 113,918 MIMIC-IV admissions, matching LAAT (0.7114) and ahead of KEPT (0.6870) while being the only fully concept-mediated model in the comparison. Interpretability validated on three architectural metrics (CSTPR 0.7042 vs. 0.1472 for a vanilla CBM).
> 🏆 Best Graduate Capstone Award · REAP Research Grant

**🧭 The Mechanism Matters** — When Knowledge Graphs Help Reinforcement Learning · *submitted AAAI 2027*
> Controlled study isolating the *injection mechanism* (state features, action masking, reward shaping) from KG quality. Structured guidance lifts solve reliability from 70% → 97% of seeds; a shuffle control collapses the gain, proving the benefit is structural. Safety is mechanism-dependent — soft shaping is robust to KG error, hard masking is brittle. Validated on six environments plus a MIMIC-IV sepsis offline-RL case study.

**🛡️ ASYM** — Measuring Trust Asymmetry in Tool-Using LLMs · *submitted EMNLP 2026*
> Introduces the **Safety Asymmetry Score**: how susceptibility to byte-for-byte identical payloads shifts across delivery channels (user message vs. tool metadata vs. tool output), across 6 production LLMs. Agent-native models are substantially more vulnerable via tool descriptions — they implicitly treat tool metadata as trusted. Causal activation patching on Llama 3.3 70B localizes the safety representation to mid-to-late layers, necessary and sufficient but non-linearly encoded.

**🧬 Controllable Electrostatic Protein Design** — *targeting PSB 2027*
> A closed-loop per-protein calibration controller that dials net charge at inference time in ProteinMPNN — no retraining. Hits targets at 7–10 MAE charge units on leakage-free clustered holdouts (vs. tens for a global bias). Transferred into BindCraft for de-novo PD-L1 binders: net charge −6.0 → ~0 with no significant loss in interface confidence.

**⚡ SpectralGAN + FRATransformer** — *submitted IEEE Trans. Power Delivery 2026* · Power transformer winding fault diagnosis via frequency response analysis; 85.7% accuracy (+23.8 pp over SVM) under 21-fold LOOCV.

**🧪 IonTheFold** — *arXiv preprint forthcoming* · Electrostatic-aware protein design fusing ESM-2, APBS, and ProteinMPNN with a novel electrostatic loss: **6.53%** gain on charged proteins, **4.14% ± 0.026** overall sequence-recovery gain. With Grinter Lab (Melbourne) and Knott Lab (Monash).

**📜 Patent (First Inventor)** — Autonomous self-landing rocket control system: custom flight computer (ARCHER 1.0), real-time sensor fusion, embedded firmware, MATLAB/Simulink simulation. Application No. 202511081185, published Nov 21, 2025 *(under examination)*.
